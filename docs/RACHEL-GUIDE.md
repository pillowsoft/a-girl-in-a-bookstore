# Your Website: How to Edit, Preview and Publish

You don't need to know any code. You tell Claude what you want, it makes the change, and you check it before anyone else sees it.

## The big idea

1. **You ask** for a change in plain English.
2. **Claude makes it** and sends you a **preview link**, a private copy of your site with the change.
3. **You look** on your phone and computer.
4. **You say "publish"** and it goes live. Nothing goes live until you say so.

## Getting started

1. Open **Claude Code** in your website folder (`a-girl-in-a-bookstore`).
2. Just start typing what you want. You don't need special commands.

## Things you can ask for

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Change the tagline on the home page to …" | Claude edits the words. |
| "Add this photo to the Travel page" (and give it the file) | Claude resizes the photo for phones and computers and puts it in. |
| "Add a new page called Podcasts" | Claude creates the page and adds it to the menu and search. |
| "Make the buttons a deeper red" | Claude changes the colors across the whole site. |
| "Fix the Instagram link in the footer: …" | Claude updates the link. |
| "Show me what it looks like" | Claude gives you a preview. |
| "Publish it" | Claude makes it live. |
| "Undo that, go back to how it was yesterday" | Claude restores the previous version. |

## Example conversation

> **You:** On the About page, change "lifelong reader" to "lifelong bookworm".
>
> **Claude:** Done. Here's your preview: `https://…pages.dev`. It may take a minute to appear. Does that look right?
>
> **You:** Yes, but make it bold.
>
> **Claude:** Updated. Same link, refresh it.
>
> **You:** Looks great, publish it.
>
> **Claude:** It's live at your website. Hard-refresh to see it.

## Photos

Give Claude the photo file (drag it into the chat or tell it where the file is). Claude will:
- make small, medium and large versions, so the site stays fast on phones,
- ask you what's in the photo, so blind visitors and Google can understand it (this is called alt text),
- remove nothing without asking.

Large camera photos are fine to give Claude. You don't need to resize them.

## Previewing

- A preview link looks like `https://something.your-site.pages.dev`.
- It takes about **1 minute** to appear after Claude sends it. If it says "not found", wait and refresh.
- Check it on your **phone** and your **computer**. Phones and desktops can look different.
- You may be asked to enter your email to see a preview. A code is emailed to you. This keeps unfinished work private.
- You can ask for as many changes as you like before publishing. The same link updates each time.

## Publishing

Say **"publish"**, **"make it live"** or **"go live"**. Claude will:
1. Double-check everything looks right.
2. Publish it.
3. Confirm your real website shows the change.

It takes about 1-2 minutes. If you don't see it, hard-refresh (**Cmd+Shift+R** on a Mac).

## If you make a mistake

Don't panic. Every version is saved. Tell Claude:
- "Undo the last change", or
- "Put the home page back the way it was on [date]".

## Things that don't work yet

Claude will tell you if you ask, but so you know:
- **The email signup and contact forms don't collect anything yet.** They say "thanks" but nothing is saved. Ask Claude to connect a real mailing list service when you're ready.
- **"Coming soon" cards** don't go anywhere until you write the story.
- **Instagram and YouTube links** at the bottom of the site are placeholders. Give Claude your real ones.

## Tips for getting good results

- Be specific: "the second paragraph on the Books page" beats "the books text".
- Say how you want it to feel: "warmer", "shorter", "more playful".
- One change at a time is easier to review than twenty.
- Always **look at the preview** before saying publish.
- If something looks wrong, just say so. Claude can try again.

## If something goes wrong

- **Preview link doesn't load:** wait a minute and refresh.
- **Claude says it can't push or isn't logged in to GitHub:** contact the person who set up your site. This is a one-time permissions fix.
- **Website looks the same after publishing:** hard-refresh (**Cmd+Shift+R**).
- **Anything else:** copy what Claude said and send it to your site helper.
